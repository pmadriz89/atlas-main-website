ATAP

Atlas Threat Assessment Program

ATAP is our primary service line. It combines threat assessment, site surveys, workplace violence prevention, executive protection planning, and practical safety training.

ATAP is built for organizations that need:

  • Clear risk findings they can act on
  • Training that holds attention and drives decisions
  • Executive protection or dignitary detail planning
  • Workplace violence prevention support
  • Site security surveys and written improvement plans
